Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

The short version. We only collect what you choose to send us. The calculator runs in your browser. We use your email to send what you asked for. We never sell your information. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who we are

worththefees.com is run by Cognizantly, Inc., the company behind Prof of Wall Street, based in Vancouver, British Columbia. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Cognizantly, Inc.

Questions about your information go to privacy@worththefees.com.

What we collect

  • Calculator inputs. Your portfolio size, fees and returns are calculated in your browser. They reach us only if you ask us to email your results.
  • Your email address. We collect it when you ask for your results or the sample report.
  • Enquiry details. If you contact us, we collect your name, how to reach you, and anything you choose to write.
  • Survey answers. Our research survey is anonymous. It does not ask for your name or email.
  • Usage counts. We count anonymous events, such as "calculator used" or "report emailed," by day, along with the country version of the site and the link or website that brought you here. These counts are not tied to you.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our servers see your IP address and browser type. We use your IP address briefly to block abuse, such as too many requests. We don't keep it with your other information.

How we use it

  • To send you the results or report you asked for.
  • To reply to you personally about your request or enquiry.
  • To send occasional insights on fees and investing, but only if you tick the box. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand which pages and links are useful, so we can improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We rely on your consent. You give it when you submit a form or tick a box. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of trusted services to run the site. They handle data only to provide their service to us.

  • Cloudflare hosts the site, stores the anonymous usage counts, and provides cookie-free visitor statistics.
  • Resend delivers the emails you request.
  • Google serves the site's fonts and hosts our email.
  • Calendly handles bookings if you click "Book a call." Calendly's own privacy policy applies there.
  • Tally hosts our anonymous research survey.

Some of these services store data outside Canada, including in the United States. Data stored there may be accessible to authorities under local law.

We never sell or rent your information. We share it only if the law requires us to.

Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser remembers two small things for convenience: the country you chose and, for this visit only, the link that brought you here. Clearing your browser data removes both.

How long we keep it

We keep emails and enquiries as long as we need them to work with you, or until you ask us to delete them. Usage counts are anonymous and kept as totals. If you unsubscribe, we stop sending insights right away.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also withdraw consent to emails at any time. Email privacy@worththefees.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

If you're in Canada and not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited to the people who need it. No website is perfectly secure, so please don't send account numbers or passwords through our forms.

Children

This site is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll say so on the site.
