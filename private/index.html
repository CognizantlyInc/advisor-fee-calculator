A letter

Every family that works with an advisor is entitled to two clear answers. Has the investing been good? And has it been worth what it costs?

Statements rarely say. I review your accounts independently, from the original records, and give you those answers plainly and in confidence. I don't manage money or sell investments. My only role is to tell you what I find.

In partnership with Ciccone McKay, I'm making this review available to the families it serves. It would be a privilege to work with you.

Amos Nadler

Amos Nadler, PhD · Founder, Prof of Wall Street

The engagement

  1. I

    A private conversation

    We meet, in person or by video, to understand your family, your accounts and what you want to know.

  2. II

    We gather everything

    With one signed authorization, we request statements and trade records directly from your advisors and custodians. You don't hunt for paperwork.

  3. III

    An independent review

    Each account is measured against a benchmark agreed with you: performance before fees, every cost, the decisions behind the results, and fit with your family's goals.

  4. IV

    Presented in person

    I present the findings to you and anyone you choose to include, with a printed, bound report and a secure digital copy.

  5. V

    Each year after

    An annual update, preparation before advisor meetings, and a direct line to me in between.

What you receive

Two clear answers

Whether the investing added value, and whether it was worth the cost, account by account.

Every cost, in dollars

Including fees inside funds and products that never appear on a statement.

The decisions behind the results

How buying, selling and position sizing affected your returns.

Questions worth asking

A short, considered list to raise with your advisors, written to strengthen the relationship.

Discretion

Your information is seen by me, and by no one else without your consent.

Records are held in encrypted storage and deleted at the end of the engagement unless you ask otherwise. A confidentiality agreement is available on request. I'm glad to work alongside your family office, accountant and legal counsel.
